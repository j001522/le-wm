Guideline
Judge the imagined frames only
Frames before the dashed line are context: the model is given the real frames and the WM panel only shows their tokenizer reconstruction. Compare each imagined WM frame with the simulation at the same time step.
Not a hallucination
Uniform blur or softness, slight colour/brightness shift, a small constant offset (a few px) that does not grow, missing texture detail. Ignore the reacher's central joint and the cup-catch string.
Invented
Something appears in the WM that is not in the simulation: an extra or duplicated object/part, an object reappearing where there is none.
Missed
Something present in the simulation is absent, vanishes or fades out in the WM.
Teleport
An object jumps discontinuously between two consecutive WM frames (implausible position change), unlike the simulation.
Warp
An object's shape, size or structure is distorted: bent or stretched arm, deformed ball, parts merging or melting.
Drifting
The object looks right but its position/pose progressively diverges from the simulation: wrong direction or speed, lagging, or frozen while the real one moves.
Onset
The first imagined frame where the problem is clearly visible (step with ←→, then O).
Unsure
Use it rather than guessing; say why in the notes.
